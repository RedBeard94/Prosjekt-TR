Add Diabetes to your home screen

Then you open the site like a normal app, without typing an address. The pages also work without internet after you have opened them once.

  1. iPhone: tap the Share button in Safari and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  2. Android: open the menu in Chrome and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Missing something? Send feedback by email (do not write any health information). The site is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your diabetes team. Everything you enter stays on this device only. See the privacy policy.
