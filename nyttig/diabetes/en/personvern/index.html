Privacy.

How we handle information in Tools for people with diabetes, with Diabetes and Diabetes simple. Last updated: 17 November 2026. This is an English translation of the Norwegian version. If the two differ, the Norwegian version applies.

Most of what you enter stays only on your own device. What is sent anywhere is described under each heading.

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Thomas Reinertsen, who runs the website as a private individual. Contact: post@reinertsengruppen.no.

What is saved on your device

My profile, the Plate, the manuals, the notes, and Settings and checklist store what you enter, for example favourites, meals, your own tasks, phone numbers, which pump and sensor you use, and your own settings from your diabetes team, only on your own device, in the browser's local storage. We do not get access to it, and nothing is sent anywhere.

See and delete: Under My profile you can see what is saved on your device. You can delete parts of it, for example pump and sensor, numbers, notes, My numbers or the Plate data. With “Delete my profile” you delete everything at once. You can also clear site data in your browser. If you use a shared device, you should delete your profile after use.

Short time: If you tap “Continue without choosing”, the browser only remembers it until you close the tab. The PIN to leave Diabetes simple is not saved.

Move and share: Under My profile you can make a code or QR code with what you choose: pump and sensor, important people and numbers, notes, My numbers and foods. The code is made and read on your device, and is not sent to us. You share it yourself. Anyone who has the code can read its content, so do not post it publicly. Foods you get from others have not been checked by health professionals, and they are marked.

Use without internet

So that the Plate and the diabetes pages work offline, the browser may store the pages themselves (text, images and fonts) on your device. These are only files from this website, no personal data. They are also removed when you delete site data or remove the app from your home screen.

The Plate, Search food and Quiz

The Plate fetches food data from the Norwegian Food Composition Database (Matvaretabellen). Matvaretabellen then receives your IP address, as with every other website you visit.

Search food: you can search Reinertsengruppen's food table (see «Barcodes and the food table» below). What you type in the search field is not sent to us and is not stored, because the search happens on your device. The rest of the page only makes links. If you tap a link, Matinfo, Oda or Meny opens in a new tab, and your search term goes to that service. They are separate services with their own privacy terms, and we have no insight into what they store. Do not write health information in the search field.

Quiz: your answers and the result are not stored. They exist only in the browser while the page is open.

Barcodes and the food table

Scanning a barcode: the camera is only used to read the barcode on your device. Pictures are not stored and not sent anywhere. You must allow the browser to use the camera, and you can say no at any time and type the barcode by hand.

Reinertsengruppen's food table: a list of items with names and carbohydrates per 100 g. The items are entered by users and checked by us. The list is fetched from our own service at Cloudflare and stored temporarily on your device, and the lookup happens there. The barcode you scan or search for is therefore not sent anywhere. As with all websites, Cloudflare technically sees your IP address when the list is fetched, but we do not store it. The numbers can be wrong, so always check against the packet.

Missing items: if you enter an item that is not in the table, it is stored first of all on your own device under My foods. We also send the barcode, the name you wrote and the number per 100 g to us, together with a random code that is made on your device. The code is not linked to a name, email or phone number. It means the same device does not count several times, and it lets us limit misuse. We do not store your IP address. The text "sent anonymously" is shown next to the field.

Why: to check new items and add them to the food table, so more people get help. The basis is the GDPR article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest). Who receives the information: the submissions are received by a service at Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare may process the request outside the EEA, on an approved transfer basis. How long: we delete submissions once they are checked, and at the latest 12 months after they were sent. Items we have approved stay in the food table without any link to the person who sent them. If you want something deleted sooner, contact us at post@reinertsengruppen.no. Do not write health information or personal names in the name field.

The feedback form

What we store: the answers you write. The form does not ask for your name or email, and you should not write your name or health information. The form service can see technical information such as the IP address.

Why and who: to make the diabetes pages better. The answers are sent through the form service Web3Forms (Web3Creative, India) to our email. Web3Forms processes them on our behalf under its data processing agreement. It uses subprocessors including Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare, and the information may be processed outside the EEA on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses (SCC). Web3Forms may keep submitted answers for up to three years. If you want them deleted, contact us and we will ask them to delete them. The email is kept with Domeneshop. We delete them no later than 12 months after we have used them.

Form for health professionals

What we store: role, name, workplace and, if you wish, email address, as well as your answers about the diabetes pages.

Why: to correct errors on the pages and to be able to ask follow-up questions. The basis is that you choose to submit it yourself (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Mention on the site: we only write that the pages have been reviewed by health professionals if you have answered yes in the form, and only with the details you have accepted (role and workplace, or also name). If you answer no, the information is used only internally.

Who receives the information: the answers are sent through Web3Forms to our email, on the same terms as the feedback form above.

For how long: we delete the email no later than 12 months after the review is finished. If you want it deleted earlier, contact us.

Health information: do not write patient information in the form. We do not need it.

Email

If you write an email to post@reinertsengruppen.no, it goes straight to us, without a form service. The email is kept with Domeneshop. We only use it to reply to you, and we delete it no later than 12 months after the matter is closed. Do not write health information.

Visitor numbers

We count how many people visit the pages, to see what is used (anonymous visit counting). The count only sends the address of the page, which part of the website it concerns, the language and whether the page was opened as an app. It uses no cookies, we do not store your IP address, and you cannot be identified. The count is sent to our portal, which is delivered through Cloudflare. Cloudflare then receives your IP address to deliver the request, as with every other website you visit. The same page is counted once per session. To know that a page has already been counted, your browser puts a small marker in session storage (sessionStorage) on your device. The marker contains no personal data, is not sent anywhere and is deleted when you close the tab. The numbers are shown only to us, in our own portal. If you have turned on "Do Not Track" in your browser, you are not counted.

The website

Our domain name and email are with Domeneshop. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may, like any server, log IP addresses. The font is stored on our own site, so it is not fetched from Google. We use no tracking or advertising services from others, and no cookies for analytics. See Visitor numbers above for our own anonymous count.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of the information we have about you, and you can ask for the processing to be restricted. Send an email to the address above. You can complain to Datatilsynet (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority).

This site is a simple aid and not a medical device. It does not replace advice from health professionals.
